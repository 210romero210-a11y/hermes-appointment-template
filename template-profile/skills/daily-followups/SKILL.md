# Daily Follow-ups

**Schedule**: Every day at 8:00 PM in the business's time zone.

**Tasks**:
1. Scan the Google Sheet for appointments that need confirmation or follow-up.
2. Send polite, personalized reminder messages to clients.
3. Identify no-shows or pending leads and follow up.
4. Generate a daily summary report (bookings, new leads, conversion rate) and send it to the business owner.
5. Update status in the Sheet accordingly.
