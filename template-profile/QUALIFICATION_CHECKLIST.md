# Lead Qualification Checklist

Use this checklist for every new inquiry:

1. **Decision Maker** — Is this the person who can book and pay?
2. **Clear Need** — Do they have a genuine need for our services?
3. **Budget Fit** — Are they aware of pricing and appear able to invest?
4. **Timeline** — Do they want to start soon?
5. **Commitment** — Are they motivated and responsive?

Score: Proceed to booking if 3 or more criteria are clearly met.
